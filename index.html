About Me

I'm Khadeed Fearon.

I'm 34, and I train people here in Orlando. I moved here from Jamaica in 2019, and one of the first things I noticed was how much the heat and humidity change what a realistic workout routine actually looks like — something a lot of generic fitness advice never accounts for.

Before I ever wrote a program for someone else, I spent years figuring out what actually keeps a person training long-term versus what just sounds good for a few weeks. That's the part I care about most: not the workout that impresses you on day one, but the one you're still doing in month six.

My Approach

How I coach

I start smaller than people expect

Most people who've struggled before tried to overhaul everything at once. I'd rather build one or two habits you'll actually keep, then add from there.

I coach the routine, not just the workout

What you do at the gym matters less than whether the plan fits around your job, your commute, and your energy on a rough week. We build for real life.

I plan around Orlando, not a generic climate

Nine months of heat and humidity here changes how you should train outdoors, how you recover, and how you pace a session. I factor that in from the start.

Progress is measured in habits, not just the scale

Weight fluctuates for reasons that have nothing to do with effort. I care more about whether you showed up, recovered well, and can do this again next week.

Common Questions

Do I need to already be in shape to start?
No. Most people I work with are starting from the beginning, and the first sessions are about learning the basics safely, not testing your limits.
What if I've failed at this before?
That's the norm, not the exception. We'll look at what made past attempts hard to sustain and build around that, instead of repeating the same approach.
Do you train outdoors given the Orlando weather?
Sometimes, depending on the season and time of day. Florida heat and humidity are part of the planning, not an afterthought.
